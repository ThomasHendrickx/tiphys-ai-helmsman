M4-P9 STEP 1: WHAT A PreToolUse HOOK ACTUALLY RECEIVES, VERBATIM

Nothing below was typed by hand. A PreToolUse hook registered for
Write|Edit|NotebookEdit|Bash appended its RAW stdin bytes, base64 encoded,
one line per firing; the payload sections are those bytes decoded. The
probe selected no fields at all, which is the difference from M4-P1's
marker: that one kept five fields it read off the payload, so session_id
and transcript_path were never captured and could not be reasoned about.

Binary: /opt/claude-code/bin/claude --version -> 2.1.273 (Claude Code)
Invocation per arm:
  claude -p <prompt> --plugin-dir <lab>/plugin --allowedTools <tool> \
    --permission-mode bypassPermissions
with IS_SANDBOX=1 exported for the child, which is the same root-gate
satisfaction M4-P1 recorded and declared (delivery/work-history/m4-p1.md:182).
The hook exited 0 on every arm below, so every arm's write LANDED and the
payloads are what a permitted call carries.

WHAT IT SETTLES, and it settles the one thing the phase was blocked on:
Write and Edit carry tool_input.file_path, ABSOLUTE. Bash carries
tool_input.command, a raw shell string, and nothing that names a file; the
heredoc arm is there because a heredoc is the form most likely to differ,
and it does not. Every arm carries a top-level cwd.
