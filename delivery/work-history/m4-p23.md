## Minute-zero record: what I read, and the one contradiction I found

Read in order: `CLAUDE.md` in full, the phase section at
delivery/plan/kernel-plan-m4.md:3133, delivery/plan/m4-conflict-pre-pass.md:1,
and delivery/verification/m4-prototype-probes.md:1.

**The dispatch and the spec name two different vocabularies, and they are two
AXES rather than one disagreement.** The dispatch says each row is `PORTED`, a
`GAP`, or `FALSE`. The spec at delivery/plan/kernel-plan-m4.md:3160 says the
closed vocabulary of three is `PORT` naming a kernel artifact, `DELETE` with a
reason, or `KEEP` with the DR-0029 side. Collapsing them would lose information
either way: `PORTED` is a FINDING about the world as it is now, and `PORT` is a
DISPOSITION about what happens next. A rule can be a `GAP` today and still be
`KEEP` (it belongs to this project's predicate, not to the kernel's process).

Resolution, recorded before any code: every row carries BOTH, as `status` in
`{PORTED, GAP, FALSE}` and `disposition` in `{PORT, DELETE, KEEP}`, and the
checker enforces the cross-consistency that makes them non-redundant.
