### VF-1. The validator cannot resolve a cross-document `$ref` as shipped

Plan step 3 requires `schemas/work-history.schema.json` to share the report
schema's `claims[]` and `fix-round[]` definitions BY `$ref` "rather than
restating them", and requires a registered test asserting "both schemas
resolve to the SAME definition object rather than to two equal ones"
(delivery/plan/kernel-plan-m3.md:2792).

As shipped at c7a7ce9, `compileSchema` builds a FRESH Ajv per schema document
and adds nothing else to it (src/validate.ts:568), and `makeAjv` withholds
`loadSchema` on purpose (src/validate.ts:526). So a `$ref` that leaves the
document fails COMPILATION, with `unresolvedRef` or `remoteRef` depending on
whether the reference carries a scheme (src/validate.ts:642).

Consequence: shipping the plan's `$ref` without a change to the compile path
would make `tiphys validate --type work-history` fail on the phase's own
example, which is acceptance criterion 1. The change is recorded under
"Key decisions" below.

### VF-2. The M2-P6 coverage checker has no CLI flag for parity mode

Acceptance criterion 4 says the M2-P6 coverage checker "run in
finding-to-outcome parity mode" exits 0 and exits nonzero on a copy with a row
deleted. `checkFindingOutcomeParity` is an exported function
(src/gates/coverage.ts:599) and the gate CLI accepts only `--result`,
`--evidence` and `--config` (src/gates/coverage.ts:704). There is no parity
subcommand and no flag that reaches that function.

Recorded rather than improvised: adding a flag would edit `src/gates/coverage.ts`,
which is NOT on this phase's declaration. How criterion 4 is discharged instead
is recorded under "Key decisions".
