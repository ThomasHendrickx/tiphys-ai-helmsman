### Step 1: the charter's product intent reaches the brief

src/commands/brief.ts:150 adds `resolveProductIntent`, and src/commands/brief.ts:193
adds `renderIntent`, called at src/commands/brief.ts:399 just before the
rendered phase. The composed brief gains a `# Intent` section with
`## Product intent` (the charter path, then the product intent verbatim) and
`## Phase intent` (the plan phase's intent verbatim).

Design decision, taken here and stated because the plan says "a declared
charter" without saying what declares one:

- A charter is DECLARED when `--charter <file>` names one (a new optional
  flag), or when any entry exists at `<cwd>/charter.yaml`. The second is the
  same file name the dual-review gate reads at a project root (CHARTER_DOCUMENT
  in src/checks.ts:4616), and cwd is where the command already looks for the
  fleet warnings file.
- Presence is judged by lstat (`classifyEntry`), so a dangling link or a named
  pipe at `charter.yaml` counts as declared and is an error, not an absence.
- A declared charter that does not read, does not decode, is not
  `kind: charter`, or has no non-blank `product-intent` makes composition exit 1
  with the charter named, and nothing is written to stdout.
- With NO charter declared, composition still succeeds and the section says so
  in one explicit sentence ("no charter declared: --charter was not given and
  <path> does not exist ..."). I chose this over failing because a fleet before
  its charter is written is a normal state (src/commands/doctor.ts:841 treats
  it as WARN, not FAIL) and every existing staged-kernel test composes with no
  charter. The sentence is visible, so it is not silent omission. If the
  orchestrator wants "no charter" to be an error, it is a one-line change at
  src/commands/brief.ts:155, and I record it as an open question below.
- The full charter schema is NOT applied by brief composition. Only the field
  it carries and `kind` are checked. `tiphys validate --type charter` owns the
  rest.

### Step 2: the delivered-outcome object

schemas/final-report.schema.json:51 adds a required `delivered-outcome`
(required list at schemas/final-report.schema.json:14): `phase-intent`
(non-blank string), `delivered` (boolean), `evidence` (array of non-blank

`additionalProperties: false`. The if/then at schemas/final-report.schema.json:89
couples `delivered: true` to `evidence` minItems 1. `delivered: false` may carry
an empty list, so an undelivered phase stays reportable. No numeric field exists
anywhere in the schema.

One measured correction during the step: the first form of the `then` branch
carried `minItems` without `type`, and the validator refused the whole schema:


Adding `"type": "array"` inside the `then` fixed it (exit 0, below).
