### What a clone can and cannot say about drain

Drain counts live worktrees under `worktrees/` and open tasks under `tasks/`
(src/cutover.ts:518). `worktrees/` is ephemeral and `resume` rebuilds it
empty, by the kernel's design ("rebuilt, not restored",
src/commands/resume.ts:18). So on this copy the worktree half of drain is
empty by construction and only the tasks half reads committed state. That
tasks half is the part that survives a reclaim. A live worktree on some other
machine running this fleet would not be visible here. Neither clone checked
here holds `state/` or `worktrees/`, so I found no live fleet home in this
container to read instead.
