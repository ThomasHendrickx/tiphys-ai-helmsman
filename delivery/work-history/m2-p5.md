## Step 3: resolution semantics

`resolveCitation` (`src/gates/citations.ts:~330-380`): external roots are
tried first (`classifyPathAgainstRoots`), then local roots, then unmatched.
A local match reads the target through `readRegularFileIfPresent`; missing
is `unresolved` naming the path, present-but-irregular is `read-error`
(bubbles to a whole-gate `error`, never silently "unresolved"), in range is
checked against `splitLines` (a trailing single newline is not counted as an
extra blank line), and a `@sha256:<64 hex>` suffix is verified by hashing
the cited line range joined with `\n`.

### CR-1019 (malformed hash suffix), CR-1023/1024/1025/1026 (grammar hardening)

The hash-suffix capture group was widened from `[0-9a-f]{64}` to

`test/citation-gate.test.ts`'s "wrong case, wrong length" test (64
uppercase hex; 63 lowercase hex).

CR-1023's three members (a colon, a backslash, and a Cyrillic letter

three: the token boundary's negative lookbehind now uses Unicode property
escapes (`\p{L}`, `\p{N}`) instead of the ASCII-only class the round-zero

trailing `-`, a decimal-looking `.5`, and a non-ASCII dash lookalike) are
