1. **DR-0012 condition 3 is only HALF mechanised, and the half is named.**

   it does not make it true.

   be a prefix of the head would satisfy the head test. I did not find a way to
   distinguish that from a real head citation without a typed field, and I am
   recording it as an open question rather than as something that cannot happen.
