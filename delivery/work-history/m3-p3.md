### D2. SCOPE GAP, reported not worked around: `templates/charter.example.yaml`

Step 4 replaces the charter schema's placeholder mode enum
(`full, standard, fast, yolo`) with this phase's mode ids
(`full, direct-pr, local-only`); criterion 4 requires `delivery-mode: yolo` to
exit 1. The SHIPPED template `templates/charter.example.yaml` declares
`delivery-mode: standard` and `assurance-tier: standard`, and three EXISTING
tests in `test/schemas.test.ts` parse that template and assert
`assert.deepEqual(validateToLines(...), [])`, an EXACT empty diagnostic list:

  - "a charter with no release-verification field is rejected naming the field"
  - "mode none with no reason is rejected naming reason and with a reason is accepted"
  - "an invented release-verification shape is rejected naming the offending property"

So the enum edit that criterion 4 requires makes the shipped template invalid
and reddens three tests this phase does not own. There is no form of criterion 4
that avoids it: keeping `standard` in the enum fails
`charter-mode-enum-matches-modes`, and keeping `yolo` fails criterion 4 directly.

`templates/charter.example.yaml` is NOT on
`delivery/plan/phase-declarations/m3-p3.json`. It is edited here (two values,
`standard` to `full`) because the phase cannot be delivered otherwise, and the
gap is REPORTED to the orchestrator rather than fixed by widening the
declaration from inside the branch. The scope gate reads the declaration from
the MERGE BASE, so only an amendment on `main` (the shape commit c2e2009 used)
can clear it.

Second, smaller: `test/fixtures/charter-no-escalation.yaml` also carries
`delivery-mode: standard`. Its test asserts by `assert.match` on one pointer and
still passes, so nothing reddens, but the fixture stops being "the valid example
with exactly one thing changed" (section 2.3 rule 1) and now carries two
defects. `test/fixtures/` is not on the declaration either. NOT edited here;
reported.
