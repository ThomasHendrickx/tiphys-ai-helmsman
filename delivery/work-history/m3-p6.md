### DECISION: how `brief-drift` is wired, and the two options that were measured

Step 3 says "mirroring M3-P2's agent-rules drift check" and the revision-3
files-to-touch note (delivery/plan/kernel-plan-m3.md:3410) says the check is

Two options were built and MEASURED in a throwaway clone rather than argued:

  `REGISTRY_ONLY_SCRIPT_GATES` at test/gate-registry.test.ts:1014, a file on no
  declaration.
- **Option B**, an entry in BOTH `gates.manifest.json` and `gate-registry.yaml`
  with `events: [pull_request]`, plus the workflow step criterion 11 requires.

Option B measured, at 086b8df in a clone, node v22.22.2, `dist/` built, bare

The two skips are the floor-gated `doctor` tests, which is what the default

Option B is taken. Its second reason is closer to the plan's intent than to its

seconds of runner time. The alternative was an `if:` on the step, which is the
shape kernel plan M3 section 2.3 rule 7 lists as a defang, so it was not taken.

### DEVIATION 1: the clean-room brief's `review-contract` is not a frontmatter field

Step 2b (delivery/plan/kernel-plan-m3.md:3355) says the clean-room brief "carries
a `review-contract` frontmatter field whose value is `criteria` or `hazard`".
It is not delivered as a frontmatter field, and the reason is mechanical rather
than a preference.

`schemas/role-brief.schema.json` declares `additionalProperties: false`
(schemas/role-brief.schema.json:8) and is M3-P5's, not on this phase's
declaration. An unknown frontmatter key therefore fails criterion 1, which
requires `tiphys validate --type role-brief` to exit 0 on both briefs. Measured:

```
$ node bin/tiphys.ts validate --type role-brief roles/clean-room-reviewer.md
EXIT=0
```

There is also a reason not to want the field. The contract is a COMPOSITION-TIME
choice: one file, one frontmatter, two contracts, and a static field cannot say
which one this composition is running. Criterion 10, which is the binding
acceptance test and is newer than step 2b, specifies exactly a `--review-contract`
FLAG on `brief compose` and says nothing about frontmatter. So the declaration is
delivered where it can be true: the composed brief carries a `review-contract:`
header line, and the non-selected contract's clause block is dropped from the
composed body, so the brief a reviewer reads names one contract and carries one
contract's instructions. Measured:

```
$ node bin/tiphys.ts brief compose --role clean-room-reviewer \
    --phase templates/plan.example.yaml --phase-id M9-P1 --review-contract criteria | sed -n 6p

$ ... --review-contract hazard | sed -n 6p
review-contract: hazard
```

The flag DEFAULTS to `criteria` for that role rather than being required, and
that is load bearing rather than lenient: test/roles.test.ts:502 composes every
brief in `roles/` with no such flag and asserts exit 0, so a required flag would
have reddened a merged phase's test.
